About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Dundee with a growing interest in software development, programming and creative technology. I'm particularly interested in building useful applications and understanding how software can solve practical problems.

Through my university coursework and personal projects, I've gained experience with Java, C, JavaScript, HTML and CSS. I've also explored object-oriented programming, data structures, file handling, debugging and testing. I enjoy learning how different parts of a program work together and improving my solutions when challenges arise.

Outside of computing, I enjoy digital illustration, animation and 3D modelling using Blender. I like combining technical problem-solving with creativity and visual design.

My experience in customer-facing and team-based roles has also helped me develop communication, organisation, adaptability and collaboration skills. I'm looking to continue developing both my technical abilities and professional experience as I work towards a career in software development.
